Free-time windows

Programme gaps vs. what is actually open. Hours are from public sources as of Sep 2026 — confirm on site. Sunset ≈ 18:15 all trip. Free-time start times are estimates from the programme, not from Albatros — the tour leader's word overrides.

Total free time: ≈ 65 h over 12 days, but only ≈ 25 h of it is daylight. The daylight sits in three places: day 7 afternoon (~4 h), day 8 afternoon (~4 h), day 9 full day (~10 h). Everything else is evenings.

Rules of thumb: Boleto Turístico (S/130, 10 days) covers Sacsayhuamán, Q'enqo, Tambomachay, Tipón, Piquillacta, Chinchero, Ollantaytambo, Moray — ask the leader whether the group ticket is yours to keep. Coricancha and Machu Picchu are separate tickets. Museo Inka closes 16:00 and on Sundays; MAP is open till 22:00 daily; Huaca Pucllana is closed Tuesdays.

After dark, by town

Lima

After dark here

☾ Lit places (360 works)

  • Circuito Mágico del Agua (Parque de la Reserva) — 13 floodlit fountains, laser show 19:15/20:15/21:30/22:30, Wed–Sun till 23:00, S/4. Great 360 subject, 15 min taxi from Miraflores. src
  • Huaca Pucllana night visit — Floodlit adobe pyramid, guided, historically Wed–Sun 19–22.
  • Malecón + Parque del Amor — Clifftop path, lit, paragliders till dusk; Larcomar below.
  • Barranco: Puente de los Suspiros + Bajada de Baños — Bohemian quarter, lit bridge and stairway to the sea, bars all around.

🍷 Famous bars & restaurants (take the group)

  • Restaurante Huaca Pucllana — Terrace dining beside the floodlit pyramid — the socialising + 360 combo. Book.
  • La Mar Cebichería (Gastón Acurio) — Famous cevichería, lunch only (closes ~17:00) — a day-2 option only if the tour ends early.
  • Panchita (Acurio) — Anticuchos + criollo classics, dinner, Miraflores.
  • Central / Maido / Astrid y Gastón — World-list restaurants; need booking months ahead and S/600+. Mention, not plan.
  • Calle de las Pizzas (Pasaje San Ramón) — Tourist-trap street off Kennedy Park but guaranteed to meet other travellers. src
  • Ayahuasca Bar (Barranco) — Pisco bar in a republican mansion — most-photographed bar in Lima.
  • La Rosa Náutica — Restaurant on a pier over the Pacific under Larcomar; expensive, ambience. src

Ollantaytambo

After dark here

☾ Lit places (360 works)

  • Plaza de Armas + Inca-lane canals — Streetlit; the fortress terraces are NOT lit. Calle Lari / Calle Horno original Inca walls under lamps.

🍷 Famous bars & restaurants (take the group)

  • Chuncho (Ollantaytambo) — Farm-to-table Andean menu, most-cited restaurant in town, plaza corner.
  • Hearts Café — Plaza café, profits to valley children’s projects; traveller meeting point. src
  • Ganso bar — Small quirky bar by the plaza, the town’s night spot.
  • Café Mayu (station) — Espresso at the rail station — mornings/day-6 return. src

Cusco

After dark here

☾ Lit places (360 works)

  • Plaza de Armas + Cathedral + La Compañía façades — Whole plaza floodlit; the best free night 360 in Peru.
  • Coricancha curved wall (Av. El Sol) — Floodlit from the street, free, any hour.
  • Hatun Rumiyoc / 12-angle stone — Lit lane, quiet after 20:00.
  • San Blas plaza + slope — Artisan quarter, lit stairs, bars.
  • Museo de Arte Precolombino (MAP) — Open till 22:00 — museum after dark.
  • Planetarium Cusco — 18:00 pick-up Plaza Regocijo, S/75, Inca astronomy + telescopes; competes with MAP. src
  • Cristo Blanco viewpoint — Lit statue above Sacsayhuamán; city lights. Taxi up + down, not on foot after dark.

🍷 Famous bars & restaurants (take the group)

  • Cicciolina — Cusco’s famous tapas/wine bar restaurant, Calle Palacio, 12:30–21:30. Book. src
  • Chicha por Gastón Acurio — Acurio’s Cusco restaurant, Plaza Regocijo, Andean classics.
  • Museo del Pisco — Pisco tasting bar, live music; sociable counter seating.
  • Limbus Restobar (San Blas) — Glass terrace over the city at dusk — the sunset drink. Arrive 17:15.
  • Paddy’s Irish Pub — Highest Irish pub, corner of the plaza, 10:00–01:00, fellow travellers. src
  • Cross Keys pub — Old British expat pub on the plaza balcony.
  • Pachapapa (San Blas) — Courtyard restaurant, wood-oven cuy/alpaca, live music some nights.
  • Mama Africa — Plaza disco, free salsa lessons ~21:00, 19:00–05:00 — if the group wants to dance. src
