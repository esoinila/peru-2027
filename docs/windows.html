Free-time windows

Programme gaps vs. what is actually open. Hours are from public sources as of Sep 2026 — confirm on site. Sunset ≈ 18:15 all trip.

Rules of thumb: Boleto Turístico (S/130, 10 days) covers Sacsayhuamán, Q'enqo, Tambomachay, Tipón, Piquillacta, Chinchero, Ollantaytambo, Moray — ask the leader whether the group ticket is yours to keep. Coricancha and Machu Picchu are separate tickets. Museo Inka closes 16:00 and on Sundays; MAP is open till 22:00 daily; Huaca Pucllana is closed Tuesdays.

Day 1 2027-03-04 Thu · evening none

Arrival. Nothing archaeological open after dark; Kennedy Park / Malecón walk only.

Day 2 2027-03-05 Fri · evening maybe

Huaca Pucllana (Day visits Wed–Mon ~09:00–16:00 (closed Tue); night visits historically Wed–Sun ~19:00–22:00 — confirm at hotel)

Huallamarca closes 17:00 → effectively out. Only evening play is a Pucllana night visit (historically Wed–Sun ~19–22, lit) — ask hotel to confirm + book on day 1. Larco is on the tour; its café stays open if the group finishes early.

Day 3 2027-03-06 Sat · late afternoon + evening good

Pinkuylluna storehouses (Daylight only; sunset ~18:15 in March. Free, unstaffed, steep 45–60 min up)

Sunset ~18:15. Pinkuylluna storehouses climb is free, 5 min from plaza, 1 h up/down — best last-light photo of the Sun Temple terraces. Sun Temple itself closes ~17:00: only if the bus arrives before 15:30 and you have a BTG.

Day 4 2027-03-07 Sun · evening none

Quillabamba plaza/market. No sites. Pool at Purakilla.

Day 5 2027-03-08 Mon · evening low

Piedras cansadas — tired stones (open landscape)

04:00 start; you will be back late. Save energy. Tired stones on the Cachiccata road are visible from the bridge in evening light if the bus passes.

Day 6 2027-03-09 Tue · evening low

Train back late afternoon. Ollantaytambo old town canals + Hatun-style walls on Calle Lari at dusk. Note: Tuesday — irrelevant here but Pucllana (Lima) is closed Tuesdays.

Day 7 2027-03-10 Wed · afternoon + evening best

Kachiqhata quarry (Daylight; 3–4 h round trip from town. Leave by 14:00 latest)
Ñaupa Iglesia / Choquequilla (Daylight; informal access from Pachar (~10 km, taxi S/30–40 each way), 20 min climb)

THE Sacred Valley window. If back by 14:00: Kachiqhata quarry round trip (3–4 h, tired stones + ramp, back before 18:15 sunset). If back later: taxi to Pachar for Ñaupa Iglesia (1.5–2 h total). Both free, daylight only, no ticket.

Day 8 2027-03-11 Thu · afternoon + evening best

Coricancha / Qorikancha (Mon–Sat 08:30–17:30; Sun 14:00–17:00 (secondary source, verify))
Museo de Arte Precolombino (MAP) (Daily 08:00–22:00 incl. holidays (official BBVA) — the late-evening option)

Coricancha until 17:30 (separate S/15–20 ticket) → Hatun Rumiyoc 12-angle stone on the walk → MAP museum is open until 22:00, so it is the after-dinner slot. Museo Inka closes 16:00 — only if Sacsayhuamán tour ends before 14:30. Planetarium 18:00 pick-up (S/75) competes with MAP.

Day 9 2027-03-12 Fri · all day best

Tipón (~07:00–18:00 daily — unverified)
Museo Inka (Mon–Fri 09:00–16:00, Sat 08:45–13:45; closed Sun)

Full day. Option A South Valley: Tipón (water channels) → Piquillacta (Wari grid) → Rumicolca gate; taxi day ~S/150–200, BTG circuit II S/70. Option B Chinchero + Huchuy Qosqo — but NO Sunday market on a Friday, so A wins unless you want the walk. Museo Inka Fri 09–16 fits the morning if you stay in town. Rainbow Mountain: PDF says Apr–Nov only — March is rainy season; treat as unavailable unless Albatros confirms.

Day 10 2027-03-13 Sat · evening none

Longest drive; Puno arrival at dusk. Sillustani (30 km) not realistic. Dreyer museum near plaza (S/15) if it is still open on arrival — unverified hours.

Day 11 2027-03-14 Sun · evening none

Back from Taquile late afternoon. Sunday: museums closed. Plaza + cathedral exterior.

Day 12 2027-03-15 Mon · morning (only if Juliaca flight is afternoon) maybe

Sillustani chullpas (~08:00–17:00 (unverified); 30 km / 45 min from Puno)

Sillustani opens ~08:00, S/15, 45 min each way from Puno, on the way to Juliaca airport. Ask the tour leader the flight time on day 10; if departure is after 13:00 a 3 h detour works with a private taxi (~S/120).
