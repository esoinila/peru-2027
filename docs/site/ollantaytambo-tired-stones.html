Piedras cansadas — tired stones

TS region

What it is

Abandoned rose-granite megaliths on the ramp west of town toward the river. Several 20–60 t blocks in the fields. Photograph the drag faces.

Look for

From the Plaza down to the Vilcanota bridge, ~25 min

☀ daylight

360 shooting: Roadside blocks.

From the encyclopedia

Local name for abandoned rose-granite megaliths on the roadway. Video: one ~80 t block the transport ministry paved around; pits, through-channel, nub. Official: men + rope + logs from Kachiqhata across the Vilcanota. Channel cites UnchartedX log-crush. GPS is region (roadside scatter).

Claims

  • Quarry path crosses Vilcanota
