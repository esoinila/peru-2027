Sacsayhuamán

HSPCPS point

What it is

Three zigzag terraces of limestone (local). PDF says 300 t corner block — inflated; look for ~8.5 m / ~120–200 t. Killke (pre-Inca) pottery under the walls is the PC leg. Rodadero is glacially polished diorite, not melted stone.

Look for

On the tour, then walk; ~20–30 min from Plaza

☀ daylight▣ enclosed

360 shooting: Chincana tunnels (Zona X) and the Amaru Machay / Temple of the Moon cave 15 min east — carved cave interiors, bring a torch.

Hours: on the tour; afternoon free
Ticket: boleto turístico — unverified price

From the encyclopedia

Documented Inca/Killke cyclopean walls above Cusco. Sorcerers of Stone (yt-nN2oeAzpAt8) start at the main gate and walk ~45 min NW through pine/eucalyptus to the Quespehuara huacas. Fortress is real; the forest huacas are a separate pin.
