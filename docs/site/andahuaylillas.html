Andahuaylillas — San Pedro church + Museo Ritos Andinos

PC point

What it is

“Sistine Chapel of the Americas”: 17th-c. church with painted ceilings and gold leaf on an Inca-era village site. Next door the small Museo Ritos Andinos shows locally excavated elongated skulls — the cranial-deformation rabbit hole, 40 min from Cusco, no Paracas trip needed.

Look for

40 km SE of Cusco past Piquillacta — the natural end of the South Valley loop.

▣ enclosed

360 shooting: Church interior is a painted box — enclosed 360 subject; photography inside is often prohibited, check.

Hours: church ~08:00–17:30, museum similar (unverified)
Ticket: Ruta del Barroco Andino ticket ~S/15–20 covers 3–4 churches; museum small fee
