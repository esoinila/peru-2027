Coricancha / Qorikancha

HSPC point

What it is

Finest HS jointing in the empire. Santo Domingo church on top is church-as-lid: 1650 and 1950 earthquakes threw down the church, not the Inca wall. Curved wall on Av. El Sol.

Look for

Walk from Plaza / Tierra Viva

▣ enclosed☾ lit at night

360 shooting: Interior andesite rooms with trapezoid niches — enclosed. Curved wall on Av. El Sol is floodlit at night from the street (free). Interior closes 17:30.

Hours: Mon–Sat 08:30–17:30; Sun 14:00–17:00 (secondary source, verify) src
Ticket: S/15–20; NOT in Boleto Turístico

Inca sun temple under Santo Domingo. Video cites the giant puma-paw carving here as a size-variant of the Quespehuara paw niches (geopolymer-stamp analogy). Temple is real; the stamp reading is overlay.
