Waqrapukara — horn fortress

PCENG point

What it is

Canchi (pre-Inca) then Inca ceremonial site at 4 300 m on a cliff above the Apurímac canyon: twin rock “horns”, three-storey fine masonry, terraces. Declared heritage 2017; the current “new Machu Picchu”.

Look for

3–4 h drive to Santa Lucía/Huayqui + 2–3 h hike each way at 4 000+ m. 12–14 h day from Cusco. Weather in March is the problem.
