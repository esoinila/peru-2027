Huaca Pucllana

PC approx

What it is

Lima-culture adobe pyramid in Miraflores — the city on top of the older city, literally. Pre-Inca adobe so PS/HS do not apply. Night tours with lights.

Look for

15 min walk from Del Pilar / Kennedy Park

☀ daylight☾ lit at night

360 shooting: Adobe pyramid floodlit on night tours (historically Wed–Sun 19–22). Walkways between brick “bookshelf” walls read well in 360.

Hours: Day visits Wed–Mon ~09:00–16:00 (closed Tue); night visits historically Wed–Sun ~19:00–22:00 — confirm at hotel src
Ticket: ~S/15 day; night tour separate; guided only
