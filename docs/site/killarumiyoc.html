Killarumiyoc — Moon stone

HSENG approx

What it is

Archaeological park at Ancahuasi: a bedrock outcrop carved with a crescent (“moon stone”), seats, channels and steps; ceremonial platforms and terraces around it. Quiet, rarely visited.

Look for

~1 h west of Cusco off the Abancay road; 10 min from Ancahuasi village.

☀ daylight

Hours: daylight, unstaffed (unverified)
Ticket: free / small local fee
