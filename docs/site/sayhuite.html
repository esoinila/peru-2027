Sayhuite / Saywite monolith

HSENGPC point

What it is

2 × 4 m andesite boulder carved into a topographic “hydraulic model”: terraces, channels, ponds, 200+ figures (pumas, lizards, people). The rabbit-hole icon of the Cusco region — scale-model-of-a-landscape claims, water-flow experiments, “lost-tech” readings. Mainstream: Inca water-cult shrine (Hemming). Also on the hill: the Rumihuasi “cut” boulders and a second sliced monolith below.

Look for

3 h west of Cusco on the Abancay road (Curahuasi). Private driver day. Combine with Tarahuasi + Killarumiyoc on the way back.

☀ daylight

360 shooting: Open hilltop; the monolith is roped off — get low with the 360 on a stick for the channel network.

Hours: ~07:00–17:00 (unverified)
Ticket: ~S/10–15 local ticket; not on Boleto Turístico
