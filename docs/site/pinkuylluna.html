Pinkuylluna storehouses

ENG approx

What it is

Opposite hillside, free, 45 min up. Best photo of the town’s Inca street grid and of the sun temple from across. The still-flowing Inca water channels in the streets are ENG in daily use.

Look for

45 min up from the Plaza

☀ daylight▣ enclosed

360 shooting: Storehouse rooms are roofless but walled — semi-enclosed with valley view.

Hours: Daylight only; sunset ~18:15 in March. Free, unstaffed, steep 45–60 min up
