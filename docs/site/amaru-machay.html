Amaru Machay — Temple of the Moon cave

HSPS approx

What it is

Carved cave 20 min NE of Sacsayhuamán: altar niches, a serpent (amaru) relief, polished steps, the skylight that lights the altar. The Cusco cave everybody films for “softened stone” claims.

Look for

Walk or 15 min taxi from Sacsayhuamán; combine with Q’enqo, Inkilltambo and Quespehuara.

▣ enclosed☀ daylight

360 shooting: Enclosed 360 gold: the cave chamber with the skylight beam. Bring a small light for the back.

Hours: daylight, unstaffed
Ticket: free
