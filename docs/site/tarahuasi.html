Tarahuasi (Limatambo)

HSENG point

What it is

Inca usnu platform with a polygonal wall of pinkish limestone whose joints form 28 “flower” patterns; lichen turns it orange. On the Chinchaysuyu royal road; Hemming calls it one of the finest walls outside Cusco.

Look for

1 h 20 west of Cusco, Limatambo. On the Sayhuite road.
