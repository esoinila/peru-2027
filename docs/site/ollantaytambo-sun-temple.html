What it is

Rose granite (not local; from Kachiqhata across the river). Lower Inca-style fieldstone vs upper megalithic course is the PC layering argument in one photo. Ruins gate 5 min from the Plaza; boleto turístico needed.

From the encyclopedia

Sorcerers of Stone (yt-rxXLg_-a1wY): rose-granite polygonal walls, scoop trains on Mohs ~7–8 faces, resonant niches. Site is real Inca/earlier fabric. Channel: stone-softening + pre-Inca Hanan Pacha. Sister to Quespehuara (same school). Cross-school Venn with Egypt granite marks.

Claims

  • Niche acoustics (overlay as sound room)

  • Named with Sacsayhuamán / Puma Punku as the three mega-sites
