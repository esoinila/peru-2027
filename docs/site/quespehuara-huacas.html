Quespehuara / Qhespewara huacas

PS region

What it is

1.5 km NW of Sacsayhuamán through eucalyptus; free; 30 min from the fortress gate. Fingerprint scoops in limestone. Photograph scoops with a scale; check whether they follow bedding planes (they do, mostly).

Look for

30 min walk from Sacsayhuamán gate (1.5 km NW)

☀ daylight

Hours: daylight; park
Ticket: free (inside park)

From the encyclopedia

Sorcerers of Stone: fingerprint scoops, pulled-soft ledges, puma-paw niches in metamorphosed limestone ~45 min NW of Sacsayhuamán. Site exists inside the park (Quespewara / Qhespewara). Mainstream: Inca/Killke/Wari tools. Channel: geopolymer or stone-softening. GPS is region — walk-time lock, not a surveyed point.

Claims

  • Fingerprint / scoop marks in hard metamorphosed limestone
  • Geopolymer-stamped-huaca reading (overlay)
