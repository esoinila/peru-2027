☾ Lit places (360 works)

  • Circuito Mágico del Agua (Parque de la Reserva) — 13 floodlit fountains, laser show 19:15/20:15/21:30/22:30, Wed–Sun till 23:00, S/4. Great 360 subject, 15 min taxi from Miraflores. src
  • Huaca Pucllana night visit — Floodlit adobe pyramid, guided, historically Wed–Sun 19–22.
  • Malecón + Parque del Amor — Clifftop path, lit, paragliders till dusk; Larcomar below.
  • Barranco: Puente de los Suspiros + Bajada de Baños — Bohemian quarter, lit bridge and stairway to the sea, bars all around.

🍷 Famous bars & restaurants (take the group)

  • Restaurante Huaca Pucllana — Terrace dining beside the floodlit pyramid — the socialising + 360 combo. Book.
  • La Mar Cebichería (Gastón Acurio) — Famous cevichería, lunch only (closes ~17:00) — a day-2 option only if the tour ends early.
  • Panchita (Acurio) — Anticuchos + criollo classics, dinner, Miraflores.
  • Central / Maido / Astrid y Gastón — World-list restaurants; need booking months ahead and S/600+. Mention, not plan.
  • Calle de las Pizzas (Pasaje San Ramón) — Tourist-trap street off Kennedy Park but guaranteed to meet other travellers. src
  • Ayahuasca Bar (Barranco) — Pisco bar in a republican mansion — most-photographed bar in Lima.
  • La Rosa Náutica — Restaurant on a pier over the Pacific under Larcomar; expensive, ambience. src
