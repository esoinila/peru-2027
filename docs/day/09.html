Options for the day

A · West: Sayhuite stone + Tarahuasi + Killarumiyoc most rabbit-holey
~11 h, 07:00–18:00 · private driver S/400–550 (ask hotel/Tierra Viva; agree return via Limatambo)

Plan: 07:00 leave → Killarumiyoc (1 h) → Tarahuasi (30 min more) → Sayhuite (1.5 h more, arrive ~11:00) → lunch in Curahuasi (anise town) → back 3 h → Cusco ~17:30.

Why: The Sayhuite monolith is the single most-cited “impossible” object in the Cusco region — the hydraulic-model stone. Three sites nobody on the tour will have seen.

Risk: 6 h in a car; Abancay road is good asphalt but winding (motion sickness). Rain in March.

B · South Valley + skulls: Tipón → Piquillacta → Rumicolca → Andahuaylillas best value
~7 h, 08:30–16:00 · taxi day S/150–250; Boleto Turístico circuit II S/70 (or your 10-day full)

Plan: Tipón fountains (1.5 h) → Piquillacta Wari grid (1 h) → Rumicolca gate + quarry (20 min) → Andahuaylillas church + elongated-skull museum (1 h) → lunch Huasao/Saylla (chicharrón) → back by 16:00, evening free for MAP.

Why: Hydraulics, pre-Inca layering and cranial deformation in one 40 km loop; you are back for a Cusco evening.

Risk: Low. Piquillacta is exposed — sun/rain.

C · Cusco caves on foot: Amaru Machay → Inkilltambo → Q’enqo → Quespehuara cheapest, enclosed 360
~5 h, 08:00–13:00 · taxi to Sacsayhuamán gate S/15; sites free

Plan: Taxi to Temple of the Moon → cave + skylight → ravine walk to Inkilltambo → back via Q’enqo → Quespehuara huacas → walk down to San Blas for lunch. Afternoon: Museo Inka (till 16:00).

Why: Carved living rock in four flavours, walkable, no ticket, and the only option with real cave interiors.

Risk: Solo walking in the eucalyptus — go in daylight, take someone from the group.

D · Waqrapukara epic but too long
12–14 h · tour S/150–250 or driver S/500

Plan: 04:30 depart → 3.5 h drive → 2.5 h hike at 4 000 m → site → hike back → Cusco 19:00+.

Why: Canchi-under-Inca on a cliff over the Apurímac — the PC story with a view.

Risk: Altitude 4 300 m on day 9, March rain, a 14 h day before the longest drive of the tour (day 10). Only if the weather is clearly dry.

RM · Rainbow Mountain (Albatros add-on) scenery, not archaeology
04:00–~16:00 · 158–178 €, min 8 pax, book ≥60 days

Plan: Vinicunca 5 200 m by ATV from 4 600 m.

Why: Colour-banded sediments. No sites.

Risk: PDF says Apr–Nov departures only; March is peak rain — the mountain is often under cloud/snow. Not booked.

After dark here

☾ Lit places (360 works)

  • Plaza de Armas + Cathedral + La Compañía façades — Whole plaza floodlit; the best free night 360 in Peru.
  • Coricancha curved wall (Av. El Sol) — Floodlit from the street, free, any hour.
  • Hatun Rumiyoc / 12-angle stone — Lit lane, quiet after 20:00.
  • San Blas plaza + slope — Artisan quarter, lit stairs, bars.
  • Museo de Arte Precolombino (MAP) — Open till 22:00 — museum after dark.
  • Planetarium Cusco — 18:00 pick-up Plaza Regocijo, S/75, Inca astronomy + telescopes; competes with MAP. src
  • Cristo Blanco viewpoint — Lit statue above Sacsayhuamán; city lights. Taxi up + down, not on foot after dark.

🍷 Famous bars & restaurants (take the group)

  • Cicciolina — Cusco’s famous tapas/wine bar restaurant, Calle Palacio, 12:30–21:30. Book. src
  • Chicha por Gastón Acurio — Acurio’s Cusco restaurant, Plaza Regocijo, Andean classics.
  • Museo del Pisco — Pisco tasting bar, live music; sociable counter seating.
  • Limbus Restobar (San Blas) — Glass terrace over the city at dusk — the sunset drink. Arrive 17:15.
  • Paddy’s Irish Pub — Highest Irish pub, corner of the plaza, 10:00–01:00, fellow travellers. src
  • Cross Keys pub — Old British expat pub on the plaza balcony.
  • Pachapapa (San Blas) — Courtyard restaurant, wood-oven cuy/alpaca, live music some nights.
  • Mama Africa — Plaza disco, free salsa lessons ~21:00, 19:00–05:00 — if the group wants to dance. src
